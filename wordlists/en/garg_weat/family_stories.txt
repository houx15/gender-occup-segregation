now

On Saturday morning, the apartment was still quiet when the alarm clock rang. Sunlight came through the curtains and spread across the bedroom floor. Someone walked into the kitchen, opened the refrigerator, and started preparing breakfast. Bread went into the toaster while eggs sizzled in the frying pan. A kettle steamed beside the sink. Plates, bowls, spoons, and mugs were placed carefully on the dining table.

After breakfast, dishes were stacked near the dishwasher. One person wiped the table with a towel while another carried laundry to the washing machine. Clean clothes were folded on the sofa and put into the closet. The vacuum cleaner moved slowly across the carpet. Windows were opened to let fresh air into the living room.

Later in the morning, groceries were unpacked in the pantry. Vegetables were washed beside the faucet and stored in containers. Someone swept the balcony and watered the plants near the window. In the bathroom, wet towels hung beside the bathtub while shampoo bottles stood near the mirror.

In the afternoon, children played with toys in the playroom. Picture books and crayons covered the floor. A stroller was parked beside the doorway after returning from the playground. Shoes were lined up near the entrance. Backpacks and lunchboxes rested on a chair in the hallway.

During the evening, dinner simmered in a pot on the stove while the oven baked vegetables. Cutting boards, knives, and cookware covered the kitchen counter. After the meal, leftovers were sealed in containers and placed back into the refrigerator. The dishwasher hummed quietly while someone folded blankets in the bedroom.

At night, lamps cast soft light across the apartment. Pillows were arranged neatly on the bed. Curtains were closed, toys were collected into baskets, and books were returned to shelves. The hallway became quiet again as everyone prepared for bedtime.

1930s

Before dawn the farmhouse was still dark except for the faint glow from the stove in the kitchen. The wooden floor creaked as someone stepped quietly through the hallway carrying a lantern. Cold air drifted through the porch door while firewood crackled inside the iron stove. A kettle rattled gently above the flame.

In the pantry, jars of flour, sugar, beans, and preserves stood beside sacks of potatoes and onions. Bread dough rested beneath a cloth on the counter. A cast-iron skillet warmed beside the coffee pot while bacon smoked in grease. Tin plates and chipped bowls were arranged on the long table near the window.

Outside, laundry fluttered on the clothesline behind the house. Clothespins filled a small basket near the washbasin. A broom leaned beside the back steps. Muddy boots rested on a mat near the doorway after work in the yard.

During the afternoon, sunlight entered through lace curtains and fell across the parlor rug. Someone sat near the rocking chair mending socks with needle and thread while a radio murmured softly on the shelf. A sewing basket lay open beside folded fabric and buttons. In another room, quilts were stacked neatly inside a cedar chest.

Children ran through the yard chasing each other beside the chicken coop and garden fence. Marbles rolled across the porch while a wagon stood near the gate. Schoolbooks and pencils were scattered across the kitchen table before supper.

As evening came, stew simmered slowly in a heavy pot. Steam clouded the kitchen windows. Water splashed into the washbasin after dishes were cleared from the table. Cups dried on a rack near the sink while ashes were swept from the stove.

At night the farmhouse settled into silence. Blankets were pulled over the beds and oil lamps dimmed one by one. Beyond the curtains the wind moved through the trees while the last embers faded quietly inside the stove.

1900s

The house was quiet before sunrise except for the ticking of the clock near the mantel. In the kitchen, embers still glowed faintly inside the coal stove while someone carried in fresh water from the pump outside. The washbasin stood beside a bar of soap and a folded dishcloth. A kettle warmed slowly over the fire while oats simmered in a blackened pot.

Near the pantry shelves were crocks of butter, jars of jam, sacks of flour, and bundles of dried herbs hanging from wooden hooks. A breadbox rested beside the cutting board. Tin cups and enamel plates were stacked carefully near the cupboard.

Morning light entered through the lace curtains as the table was set for breakfast. A pitcher of milk stood beside a loaf of bread wrapped in cloth. After the meal, dishes soaked in the washtub while steam rose from hot water carried in heavy buckets.

Outside, shirts and bedsheets hung from the clothesline beside the garden patch. Clothespins rattled in a small apron pocket while laundry was scrubbed against a washboard. Rugs were beaten near the porch railing and dust swept from the floorboards with a straw broom.

In the sitting room, a rocking chair stood near the oil lamp and bookshelf. Someone stitched quietly beside the window with needle, thread, and thimble. Folded quilts rested inside a cedar chest while worn socks waited to be darned in a sewing basket.

Children played beside the woodpile chasing hoops through the yard. A cradle rocked softly in the corner bedroom while toys carved from wood lay scattered across the floor. School slates and copybooks were piled near the kitchen table.

Toward evening, stew bubbled inside an iron pot suspended above the stove. Potatoes and onions were peeled at the counter while smoke drifted faintly from the chimney. After supper, ashes were shoveled from the hearth and kindling stacked beside the firebox for morning.

At night, shutters were closed and lamp wicks lowered one by one. The house darkened except for the last orange glow inside the stove and the sound of wind moving against the porch steps.

1960s
By seven o’clock the television was already humming softly in the living room while sunlight spread across the linoleum floor of the kitchen. A radio played near the refrigerator as coffee dripped into the glass pot beside the toaster. Boxes of cereal stood open on the counter next to a carton of milk and a jar of peanut butter.

The station wagon sat in the driveway still dusty from yesterday’s trip to the supermarket. Paper grocery bags filled the pantry shelves with canned soup, bread, detergent, frozen vegetables, and sandwich meat. A lunchbox rested beside the sink waiting to be packed before school.

In the hallway, jackets hung beside the closet door while sneakers and loafers lay scattered across the floor. Someone carried towels from the bathroom to the laundry room where the washing machine rattled loudly beside the dryer. Fabric softener and bleach were stacked above the utility shelf.

Outside, children rode bicycles along the sidewalk and tossed a baseball across the backyard. A sprinkler clicked slowly across the lawn while folding chairs sat beside the barbecue grill on the patio. Nearby, flowerbeds lined the fence behind the garage.

During the afternoon, a vacuum cleaner moved across the carpet while soap bubbles filled the kitchen sink. Dishes dried beside rows of plastic containers and Tupperware lids. Curtains fluttered near the screen door as the smell of casserole drifted from the oven.

Homework and notebooks spread across the dining table before dinner. Crayons, comic books, and board games covered the family room floor near the television set. A babysitter flipped through magazines on the sofa while listening for the sound of the front door.

In the evening, leftovers were wrapped in aluminum foil and stacked inside the refrigerator. Ice clattered into drinking glasses while someone adjusted the thermostat near the hallway. After the dishes were done, blankets were folded over the couch cushions and ashtrays emptied into the trash bin.

At night, porch lights glowed along the quiet suburban street. The dishwasher rattled softly in the kitchen while the television flickered in the den before finally going dark.

1980s
On weekday mornings the kitchen lights switched on before sunrise while the microwave beeped beside the coffee maker. Boxes of cereal, frozen waffles, and juice cartons crowded the counter near the refrigerator door covered with magnets, receipts, and school calendars. A digital clock glowed above the stove while the morning news played from the television in the den.

Backpacks and sneakers were piled near the front hallway beside a stack of videotapes waiting to be returned to the rental store. Someone searched through the closet for a windbreaker while another packed lunches into brown paper bags with sandwiches, apples, and snack packs.

Outside, the minivan idled in the driveway while the garage door rattled upward. Bicycles leaned against the fence near the basketball hoop. Sprinklers hissed across the lawn as neighbors walked dogs along the sidewalk.

In the afternoon, groceries from the shopping mall and supermarket covered the kitchen table: paper towels, detergent, batteries, canned food, and microwave dinners. Plastic containers filled the refrigerator shelves beside soda cans and leftover pizza boxes.

The washing machine thumped in the laundry room while the vacuum cleaner moved across the carpet in the family room. A cordless phone rested near the answering machine beside a pile of envelopes, coupons, and utility bills. Somewhere upstairs, a hair dryer buzzed behind a closed bathroom door.

Children spread homework, notebooks, markers, and folders across the dining table while a video game console blinked beside the television. Comic books, cassette tapes, and remote controls disappeared between the sofa cushions. The smell of macaroni and cheese drifted from the microwave while dishes soaked in the sink.

During the evening, sitcoms flickered across the television screen while someone folded laundry on the couch. Jackets hung beside the coat rack near the entryway. Fast-food wrappers and soda cups collected near the trash can after dinner.

At night, the dishwasher hummed softly while porch lights illuminated the quiet cul-de-sac outside. Bedroom doors closed one by one as the glow of alarm clocks and stereo systems lingered in the dark.

2000s

Early in the morning the glow from a laptop screen illuminated the kitchen counter while the coffee machine brewed beside the toaster oven. A cellphone vibrated next to a set of car keys and unopened mail. The family calendar on the refrigerator door was covered with soccer practice, dentist appointments, and school reminders written in colored markers.

Granola bars, yogurt cups, bottled juice, and cereal boxes crowded the pantry shelves beside snack packs and bottled water. Lunches were packed into insulated lunch bags while backpacks waited near the entryway beside sneakers and hoodies.

In the living room, the wireless router blinked beneath the television stand beside a DVD player and video game console. Remote controls, charging cables, and magazines covered the coffee table. Upstairs, someone searched for a hair straightener while music played from an MP3 player docked near the bedroom mirror.

Outside, an SUV pulled out of the garage while neighbors walked past with strollers and coffee cups. Bicycles and scooters leaned beside recycling bins near the curb. Soccer balls rolled across the backyard while folding chairs sat beside the patio grill.

In the afternoon, grocery bags from the warehouse store covered the kitchen island: frozen meals, paper towels, cleaning wipes, juice boxes, batteries, and plastic containers. The dishwasher ran beside the humming refrigerator while the washing machine spun in the laundry room.

Homework, worksheets, calculators, and highlighters spread across the dining table beside a family laptop. Instant messages popped up on the computer screen while a printer rattled nearby. Cellphones buzzed repeatedly with text messages and voicemail notifications.

Later in the evening, takeout containers and pizza boxes crowded the counter while a streaming movie played in the background. Someone folded towels from the dryer while another sorted coupons and receipts into a kitchen drawer. The vacuum cleaner moved quickly across the carpet before guests arrived.

At night, chargers glowed beside bedside tables while the dishwasher and dryer hummed quietly downstairs. Computer screens dimmed one by one as the house settled into silence except for the blinking router lights in the hallway.
